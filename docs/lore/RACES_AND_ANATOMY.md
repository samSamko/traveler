# Lore Bible — Races & Physical Anatomy

## 1. Vaelorian (Ash-Born Inheritors)
- **Skin Range**: Pale Ivory, Warm Olive, Weathered Tan, Bronze, Deep Umber.
- **Hair Range**: Raven Black, Chestnut, Ash-Blonde, Copper-Red, Iron-Grey.
- **Eye Range**: Storm-Grey, Hazel, Amber, Dark Brown, Pale Blue.
- **Distinguishing Marks**: Kiln-Ember Brand, Imperial Signet-Callus, Soot-Stained Knuckles.
- **Cultural Ties**: Honored by `@npc:captain_vane` and Iron-Vow patrols; blamed by elder folk for cracking the First Kiln.

## 2. Ael-Thalor (Dusk-Woven Exiles)
- **Skin Range**: Moon-Pale, Silver-Ivory, Dusk-Ash, Frost-Grey, Starlit Alabaster.
- **Hair Range**: Starlight Silver, Pale Gold, Ash-White, Midnight Blue, Obsidian.
- **Eye Range**: Liquid Silver, Pale Violet, Frost-Blue, Star-Gold, Mist-Grey.
- **Distinguishing Marks**: Silver Vein-Lines, Star-Script Ink, Hollow Collarbones, Ageless Stillness.
- **Cultural Ties**: Exiled from `@place:petrified_starwood`; can calm `@enemy:star_stag` and read pre-Shattering Star-Script in `@place:weeping_barrow`.

## 3. Durnfolk (Basalt-Kin of the Deep Forges)
- **Skin Range**: Slate-Grey, Iron-Tan, Weathered Ochre, Basalt-Brown, Soot-Bronze.
- **Hair Range**: Coal Black, Rust-Red, Forge-Grey, Bronze-Brown, Soot-White.
- **Eye Range**: Ember-Amber, Flint-Grey, Iron-Brown, Smoky Quartz, Deep Copper.
- **Distinguishing Marks**: Slate Knuckle-Ridges, Basalt Cheek-Striations, Forge-Soot Beard, Iron-Ringed Braids.
- **Cultural Ties**: Builders of `@place:drowned_forge` and counterweight vaults in `@place:weeping_barrow`; command `@enemy:forge_automaton`.

## 4. Grixi (Brass-Warren Guild-Clerks)
- **Skin Range**: Mossy Olive, Soot-Green, Tarnished Brass, Chalk-Sage, Damp Lichen.
- **Hair Range**: Soot Black, Mire-Brown, Copper-Tarnish, Chalk-Grey, Moss-Dark.
- **Eye Range**: Lantern-Yellow, Tarnished Gold, Soot-Orange, Bog-Green, Amber-Bright.
- **Distinguishing Marks**: Notched Ears, Lantern-Sheen Pupils, Alchemical Acid-Stains, Brass Guild-Pin.
- **Cultural Ties**: Citizens of `@faction:under_warren`; unlock hidden ledger wares at `@place:cinderford`, squeeze through chitin-flues, and harvest `@item:husk_gland`.
