# Races, Stature & Systemic Friction

## 1. Playable Southern Races (Early-Medieval South)
Racial hostility in the south is systemic, not incidental. Old blood-feuds, border raids, and suspicion follow exiles even past the Black Stone Gate.

1. **Human** (`vaelorian` internal ID):
   - Short-lived, stubborn folk of the fragmented southern kingdoms. Dominant in southern towns, deeply suspicious of non-humans, yet quick to turn on their own deserters, debtors, and heretics.
2. **Elf** (`ael_thalor` internal ID):
   - Long-lived, silver-eyed outsiders from dwindling highland woods. Viewed with superstitious dread by human villagers; acutely sensitive to cold resonance and the spreading stillness beyond the Gate.
3. **Dwarf** (`durnfolk` internal ID):
   - Stout, broad-shouldered smiths and stone-wardens from deep southern holds. Clannish, unforgiving of oath-breakers, and physically resilient against cold, poison, and blight.
4. **Goblin** (`grixi` internal ID):
   - In the south, goblins are **mostly barbaric** tunnel-raiders and scavengers hunted on sight. **The player is a rare exception**—a sharp-eared, lantern-eyed individual who learned speech, trade-craft, and alchemy, enduring constant prejudice wherever southern survivors gather.

## 2. Non-Playable Brutal Savages
- **Orc-Kin** (`orc_kin` creature type):
  - 100% barbaric, brutally savage, and almost animal-like in their cruelty and pack violence. They build no lasting cities, respect no parley, and raid both southern borderlands and the northern approaches with relentless ferocity.

## 3. The Lost Northern Builders (Unknown Race)
- The civilization beyond the Black Stone Gate was **not** human, elf, dwarf, or goblin.
- Their true name and origin are unknown, but their ruins prove they were **noticeably taller** than every southern race: doorways tower overhead, marble steps are cut for longer strides, and shattered statues depict elongated, solemn figures in draped late-imperial robes.
