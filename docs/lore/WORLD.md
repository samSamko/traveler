# Lore Bible — World & Cosmology

## The Gloaming Reach
A mist-bound border valley built atop subterranean root-veins cracked open seven generations ago when the ancient Crown-Wrought ignited the **First Kiln of Aethel** using harvested soul-amber (**Vesper**).

## The Husk-Tide & Third Drowning Toll
From the breached deep conduits rises the **Husk-Tide**: a slow calcifying curse of chalk-white roots, chitin-ash, and resonant bronze chimes. Beneath **The Sunken Belfry**, **Malakor, the Choir-Husk** gathers breath for the **Third Drowning Toll** on Day 14, which will petrify every living creature in the Reach unless interrupted or mastered.

## Binary Customs & Factions
The Reach preserves rigid ancient social divides between men and women (`male` / `female`):
- **The Iron-Vow Garrison** (`faction:iron_vow`, `@place:cinderford`): Martial frontier remnant clinging to patriarchal levy law; respects Vaelorian blood-charters and martial defiance.
- **Matrons of the Silken Cistern** (`faction:silken_cistern`, `@place:silken_cistern`): Blind subterranean weavers guarding counter-harmonic looms; natively welcome women behind their silk veil and demand proof of faith or blood-oaths from men.
- **Brass-Warren Guild** (`faction:under_warren`, `@place:cinderford`): Subterranean Grixi contract-clerks and alchemical smugglers maintaining black-ledger trade routes.
- **Resonance of the Pale Choir** (`faction:husk_choir`, `@place:sunken_belfry`): The calcifying harmonic current linking Malakor, Choir Acolytes, and hollowed husks.
