# World Canon — The Far North & The Black Stone Gate

## 1. What Every Southern Traveler Knows (Observable Legend)
In every southern city, town, and village, stories persist of the Far North: looming mountains with castles carved into the stone, tundras so cold the air burns the lungs, barren taigas where you can hear your own heartbeat for miles, jagged rocky hills that chew through boot-leather, and grim forests whose roots seem to grip the earth.

Criminals with nowhere left to hide flee north. Soldiers without a cause, ruined peasants, and desperate or mad wanderers march into the cold and are never heard from again. The farther north one travels, the stronger the compulsive urge to keep going grows—and the harder the land itself rejects the living. Crops fail, food spoils, animals turn vicious, and sickness spreads. No southern lord or king has ever held a settlement there; only scattered hermits, witches, heretics, apostates, and outcasts endure the madness.

Past a certain point, the mountains narrow to a single enormous gate carved of intricate black stone. With each step closer, the air freezes and the compulsion to cross turns violent. Some who touch the stone freeze solid where they stand; others force the giant doors apart and vanish into whatever lies beyond.

## 2. Contrast of Eras & The One-Way Threshold
- **The Early-Medieval South**: Fragmented feudal realms, rough timber-and-mud settlements, and systemic inter-racial hostility. Suspicion, old blood-feuds, and survival scarcity define daily life.
- **The One-Way Threshold**: Crossing the Black Stone Gate is irreversible. Once a traveler passes through, the gate vanishes behind them, severing any retreat to the south and locking them into the Northern Expanse.
- **Beyond the Gate (Constantinian / Late-Roman Ruin)**: A forgotten, overgrown land built on a monumental scale—wide marble avenues, towering triumphal arches, broken aqueducts, domed halls, and carved waystones—constructed by a mysterious, taller lost civilization.

## 3. Currency: Onyx Coins
The lost northern civilization minted uniform, sharp-edged coins of an indestructible black onyx-like material. In the south, common coin and barter rule; beyond the Black Stone Gate, these indestructible **Onyx** coins are the sole currency hoarded by trapped scavengers, hermits, and remnants.

## 4. The 4-Phase Lunar Cycle
Every 12 days, the moon completes a 4-phase cycle (3 days per phase) that governs survival and dread across the Northern Expanse:
1. **New Moon — The Severed Thread**: Outward pull weakens, residual warmth drops, sightlines narrow, and cold + Defilement leakage bite harder at night.
2. **Waxing Moon — The Silver Tugging**: Growing lunar light sharpens the compulsive northward pull; more desperate drifters and scavengers press deeper into the ruins as old conduits begin to hum.
3. **Full Moon — The Lunar Eye**: Peak alignment. Overgrown marble plazas sit under stark pale light, Defilement spikes outdoors at night, and the veil thins to its narrowest point.
4. **Waning Moon — The Ash-Settling**: The pull recedes into heavy, sluggish quiet. Scavengers strip what remains while rations and warmth strain under late-cycle attrition.

## 5. WRITER-ONLY LATE-GAME SECRETS (FORBIDDEN IN EARLY PROSE)
> Do NOT reveal or hint directly at these in the intro, early areas, or casual NPC dialogue. Surface only through deep-ruin inscriptions, late-game dossiers, and extreme Defilement / Full Moon triggers.

- **The Cataclysmic Truth**: The northern civilization discovered a meteoric fragment and used it as a philosopher's stone for transmutation and industry, unaware it sustained its power by draining memory, consciousness, and selfhood. Their final attempt to transmute their farthest capital into **pure silver** triggered total collapse, locking the populace into vacant stupor before rot set in.
- **The Moon Was Never Empty**: Deep inscriptions beyond the gate indicate the meteoric stone was not ordinary iron, but a shed scale, seed, or sensory organ of an entity dwelling on or within the moon.
- **Why the Silver Capital Failed**: Trying to force permanent perfection using a fragment tuned to lunar cycles mirrored its lunar source—stripping individual selfhood into a uniform, hollow echo across the capital.
- **Ultra-Rare Lunar Entities**: Strictly scarce cosmic anomalies (absent from normal patrols), manifesting only under Full Moon alignment, high Defilement, or deep-ruin triggers.
