# Characters, Exiles & Survivor Factions

## 1. Playable Catalysts (Why You Fled North)
- **Ashen Blade**: Disgraced soldier or oath-breaker fleeing execution or lordly prosecution.
- **Root-Scholar**: Exiled researcher or heretic driven north by obsession with the forbidden northern signs and waystones.
- **Barrow-Stalker**: Debt-ridden tomb-robber or guide chasing rumors of indestructible onyx coin beyond the mountains.
- **Shrine-Penitent**: Apostate or cursed penitent cast out from southern parishes into the white northern wastes.

## 2. Survivor Factions Beyond the Gate
Since crossing the Black Stone Gate is one-way, anyone encountered beyond it is trapped—whether newly arrived southern exiles, maddened hermits, or rare survivors of the northern collapse:
- **Trapped Garrisons & Deserters**: Desperate armed bands holding collapsed watchtowers or fortified road-camps, hoarding firewood, salt, and onyx coin.
- **Apostate Circles & Hermits**: Witches, flagellants, and outcasts who endure the mental tug of the north by grafting bone, salt, or bronze wards into their own flesh.
- **Vacant Remnants**: Rare figures who survived near the edge of the Pure Silver Capital—speaking in flat, hollowed cadences, slowly losing names and memories to Defilement.
