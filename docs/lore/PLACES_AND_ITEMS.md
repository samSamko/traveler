# Geography, Ruins & Onyx Economics

## 1. Progression Beyond the Black Stone Gate
1. **The Lone Camp (Hub)**: Your solitary camp established just inside the frost-bitten valley beyond the vanished Black Stone Gate. From here you scout outward (`Explore`), travel to discovered destinations, brew draughts, repair armor, and manage your Stash.
2. **The Northern Expanse (10 Scaling Frontier Areas)**: Frosted hills, jagged stone passes, ruined Late-Roman aqueducts, wide cracked marble boulevards, sunken orchards, and overgrown plazas inhabited by beasts, orc-kin packs, husks, and trapped outcasts.
3. **Secret Sub-Areas & Dungeons**: Hidden side-ledges, flooded sluiceways, catacomb galleries, and subterranean vaults built to taller-than-human proportions—navigated room-by-room with persistent switches, traps, and locks.
4. **The Pure Silver Capital (Deep End-Game Horizon)**: At the farthest north sit monumental Constantinian domes and silent avenues where the northern collapse struck hardest—preserved by cold and ash.

## 2. Currency & Scarcity
- **Onyx Coins**: Sharp-edged, indestructible black coins stamped with unrecognizable geometric dies by the lost taller builders. Trapped merchants, hermits, and scavengers beyond the Gate trade strictly in Onyx coins alongside food rations and lamp fuel.
- **No Unfounded Safe Havens**: No southern king holds territory here. Even survivor outposts (`town` / `special`) lack `Explore` and merely offer shelter, tense NPC barter, and repairs while rations dwindle.
