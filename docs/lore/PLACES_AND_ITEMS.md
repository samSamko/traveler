# Lore Bible — Places & Items

## The Nine Locations
1. `place:cinderford` — **Cinderford Keep**: Timber border settlement, Iron-Vow muster yard, Quartermaster Harken, Ashen Flagon inn, Granary crisis (`Day 8` fall trigger if neglected).
2. `place:kings_causeway` — **The Old King's Causeway**: Broken Imperial flagstones, milestones, courier ditch, Kale encounter.
3. `place:weeping_barrow` — **The Weeping Barrow**: Pre-Shattering tumulus holding `@item:echo_shell`, Sir Vorne's oath tablet, First Kiln Cipher frieze, and Durnfolk counterweight vault (`@item:runic_warhammer`).
4. `place:ash_fen` — **The Ash-Fen & Spore-Mire**: Bog herb banks (`@item:bog_herb`), stepping stones, sunken shrine, Grixi mire vents.
5. `place:silken_cistern` — **The Silken Cistern**: Subterranean loom sanctuary, `@npc:matron_veyra`, Quiet Pool, Vesper Font temptation, Seal of Silence.
6. `place:petrified_starwood` — **The Petrified Star-Wood**: Fossilized white forest, Resin Grove (`@item:ash_resin`), Heart-Glade (`@enemy:star_stag` -> `@item:star_glaive`), Western Root Conduit.
7. `place:drowned_forge` — **The Drowned Forge-Vault**: Flooded Durnfolk basalt halls, `@enemy:forge_automaton` -> `@item:basalt_plate`, Runic Brazier honing, Abyssal Sluice-Valve.
8. `place:crown_watchtower` — **Crown-Gate Watchtower**: Shattered parapet overlooking the Belfry, `@npc:sir_vorne_npc` vigil (`@item:bell_cleaver`).
9. `place:sunken_belfry` — **The Sunken Belfry**: Drowned amphitheater housing `@enemy:malakor` and the 3 endgame branches.

- **Relic Gear**: `@item:runic_warhammer`, `@item:star_glaive`, `@item:bell_cleaver`, `@item:vesper_needle`, `@item:basalt_plate`, `@item:chitin_mantle`, `@item:echo_shell`, `@item:silken_veil`.
